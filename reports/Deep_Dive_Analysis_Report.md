# Sales Performance and Customer Insights

## Deep-Dive Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

### 1. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales performance, identify high-revenue products and cities, and develop an interactive Power BI dashboard to support business decision-making.

### 2. Dataset Overview

The dataset contains 1,000 sales records and 12 columns, including order date, customer details, product category, quantity, unit price, city, and total sales. The analysis covers January 2025 to January 2026.

### 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI                 |           Value | Business Relevance                        |
| ------------------- | --------------: | ----------------------------------------- |
| Total Revenue       | ₹139,399,439.65 | Measures total sales revenue              |
| Total Unique Orders |             992 | Measures the number of distinct orders    |
| Average Order Value |     ₹140,523.63 | Measures average revenue per unique order |
| Total Quantity Sold |           5,435 | Measures the total quantity sold          |
| Unique Customers    |             947 | Measures the number of distinct customers |

### 4. Key Findings

**Category Performance:** Electronics is the leading category, generating approximately ₹5.08 crore, or 36.4% of total revenue. Education ranks second, generating approximately ₹2.50 crore.

**Product Performance:** Laptops and mobiles are the highest-revenue products in the displayed results, generating approximately ₹2.54 crore and ₹2.53 crore, respectively.

**City Performance:** Patna is the highest-revenue city at approximately ₹1.93 crore, followed by Kolkata and Bengaluru.

**Monthly Performance:** Revenue varies across months. The latest recorded date is January 1, 2026, so January 2026 may represent an incomplete month and should not be compared directly with complete months.

### 5. Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes KPI cards, category and product revenue charts, a city revenue chart, a monthly revenue trend, and interactive filters for exploring the data.

### 6. Business Recommendations

1. Monitor inventory and demand for high-revenue products.
2. Investigate the factors contributing to Electronics category performance.
3. Study customer purchasing patterns in high-revenue cities.
4. Monitor monthly revenue trends to identify opportunities and unusual changes.
5. Use interactive filters to compare sales across categories, cities, and time periods.

### 7. Data Quality and Limitations

The initial data-quality review found 20 missing Age values, 13 missing City values, zero exact duplicate rows, and eight repeated Order_ID values. Repeated Order_ID values were retained because they may represent multiple records associated with an order.

Missing values should be handled consistently during data preparation. The dataset is limited to the records provided and does not establish the reasons behind sales differences.

### 8. Conclusion

The analysis identifies Electronics as the leading revenue category and highlights differences in product and city performance. The interactive Power BI dashboard provides a visual way to explore these results and support data-informed business decisions.
